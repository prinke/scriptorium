Medieval readers wrote in their margins. This theme draws in them instead.

Commonplace
: A passage copied out to keep, sorted under a heading.

Lacuna
: A gap in a manuscript where text has been lost. Also the theme’s 404 page.

> Some books are to be tasted, others to be swallowed, and some few to be chewed and digested.
> <cite>Francis Bacon, “Of Studies”</cite>

1. Ordered lists use gold numerals.
2. Nested lists work too:
   - like this,
   - and this.

- [ ] Are supported
